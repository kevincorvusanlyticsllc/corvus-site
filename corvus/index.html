Corvus

A voice-first AI assistant for the trading desk. Speak in plain language; Corvus answers out loud in seconds, from live data, and writes the full read on screen.

See it run

What it does

Listens
Questions, chart commands and order tickets in ordinary speech, picked out from the noise of a working desk. It knows its own voice from the trader’s.
Reads
Positions, quotes, option chains, factor exposures and the market regime, pulled live as they are asked for, with the news and the accounts the trader follows read against the book.
Answers
Out loud and to the point, with longer reads written on screen. It draws trend lines and levels on the chart when asked, and every figure it gives comes from live data.
Learns
Phrasings the trader confirms, new skills and the day’s context carry forward. New requests become skills, learned in an after-market training session.

How it’s built

Corvus matches each job to an external AI model: a fast model for reading new phrasings and keeping its notes, a core model for the conversation and its tools, and a larger expert model for hard questions and nightly training. Phrasings it has already learned are handled locally, with no model call at all. A daily log and a rolling summary give it memory across the day and across restarts.

Built for a live desk

Order tickets are read back and checked against size and risk caps before anything goes out. A dry-run mode routes nothing, and one word halts everything. Corvus runs beside the trading platform on the same machine, and stays light while it does.

Who it’s for

Independent traders and small funds that want desk-grade tools without building them. Corvus runs every day on Corvus Analytics’ own trading desk, and we’re taking on software work with traders and funds who want to talk about it.
