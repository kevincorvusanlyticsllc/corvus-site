Flagship · In private use

Corvus

A voice-first assistant for the trading desk, built and used here every day. Ask it about the book, the tape or a chart in plain speech, and it answers out loud in seconds, from live data. It keeps learning on the job, and when a question needs more, it calls on larger external AI models.

  • Listens

    Questions, chart commands and order tickets in ordinary speech, picked out from the noise of a working desk.

  • Reads

    Positions, quotes, option chains, factor exposures and the market regime, pulled live as it is asked.

  • Answers

    Out loud and to the point, with the full read written on screen. It draws on the chart when asked, and every figure it gives comes from live data.

  • Learns

    New requests become skills, learned after the close. Confirmed phrasings, new skills and the day’s context stick, and harder questions go to larger external models.
